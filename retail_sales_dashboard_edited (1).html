About this project

This dashboard presents the results of a retail sales analysis built for a fictional client, QuickMart Retail, and visualised here and in Power BI.
  • Coverage: 9,800 records, Jan 2015 – Dec 2018
  • Forecast: 6-month Holt-Winters projection
  • Presentation layer: Power BI and this web dashboard

Key insights

  • West and East together bring in about 61% of revenue; South is the weakest region at 17.2%.
  • The three categories are balanced, so the business does not depend on a single product line.
  • Sales peak in Q4 (Nov–Dec) and dip in Jan–Feb every year.
  • The Canon imageCLASS 2200 Advanced Copier ($61,599.82) earns almost 3× the second-ranked product.

Recommendations

  • Jan–Feb: reduce replenishment orders by 15–20% versus Q4 2018 and avoid major launches.
  • March: stock top-performing items 3–4 weeks ahead of the forecast peak.
  • Apr–Jun: keep inventory steady and run targeted promotions in the South region during May–June.
  • Planning: use the H1 total of $354,652 as the benchmark for variance analysis.
